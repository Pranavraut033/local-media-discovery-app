# Local Media Discovery App v1.0.0

The first stable release: a private, swipe-to-browse app for your own photo
and video library — no cloud, no accounts, no tracking. Everything runs on
your machine and stays on your network.

**What's new:**

- **Swipe through your media** like a personal, ad-free Reels — feed view,
  gallery view, and a full-screen viewer for photos and videos.
- **Browse remote libraries too.** Connect rclone or WebDAV sources (NAS,
  cloud drives you control) alongside your local folders, with automatic
  syncing and self-healing connections.
- **A smarter feed.** Media surfaces based on what you haven't seen yet, with
  a mix of sources so one folder doesn't dominate.
- **Organize as you go.** Like, save, and hide files or whole folders — your
  preferences are private to your account.
- **Runs as a real desktop app.** Packaged with Electron: launches with one
  click, sets itself up on first run, and can reveal files in Finder or open
  them in your default apps.
- **Back up your data locally** with built-in export/import — still no
  cloud involved.
- **Works well on phones** on your LAN too, with a mobile-friendly PWA layout.

Simple 6-digit PIN login — nothing to sign up for, nothing leaves your network.
